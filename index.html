How it works

Three steps, and the car keeps its own record.

Step one
Add the plate

Emirate, code, number. Brand, model and odometer if you have them — the rest can wait.

Step two
Log the visit

Garage, odometer and the lines you paid for. Attach the invoice photo and it stays with the record.

Let it add up
